About the Company

Operationally disciplined facility services built for scale.

Coastal Facility Services is a commercial-only contractor purpose-built to serve property managers, facility managers, and operators of large commercial real estate portfolios across Southern California.

From routine janitorial programs and day porter coverage to specialty floor coatings and post-construction cleanup, our crews operate to documented standards, measurable KPIs, and the kind of operational rigor enterprise clients expect.
